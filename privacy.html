Privacy Policy

Version 1.1 · Updated October 20, 2026 · For the Tallysprig V6 prototype

Tallysprig is a standalone, installable food journal hosted as a static website. This policy explains what the current app stores and when third-party services are contacted. The project does not offer accounts or cloud synchronization.

1. Information kept on your device

When you enter foods, Tallysprig stores meal dates, names, portions, estimated calories and macronutrients, custom foods, favorites, and your optional daily energy reference, appearance preferences (light/dark mode, Classic/Health Mode and chart display), optional macro reference values and any health profile details you choose to enter (age, height, weight, equation input, activity level) in your browser's local storage. It also stores scan cache information, API-rate pacing information, full-backup timestamps, and the date and versions of the documents you acknowledged. Health Mode preferences and profile fields are stored locally under a separate app storage key; they are not sent to Open Food Facts for calculations.

This information is used to operate the food journal and is not automatically uploaded to a Tallysprig-owned server. It remains available to other people with access to your device or browser profile.

2. Third-party connections

  • GitHub Pages: Your device connects to GitHub's hosting infrastructure to load the website. The hosting service may process technical data such as IP addresses, timestamps, and request details according to its own privacy practices. GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Open Food Facts: When you ask Tallysprig to look up a barcode, that barcode is sent to Open Food Facts. Its servers may receive IP addresses and ordinary request metadata. Your meal history is not sent in this request. Open Food Facts website.
  • jsDelivr: If you activate camera/photo barcode scanning, a barcode-scanning library may load from jsDelivr. That service may receive normal browser request information. The scanning itself runs in the browser; Tallysprig's code does not intentionally upload camera frames to its own servers.

3. Camera and photos

Tallysprig requests camera access only after you choose the camera-scanning feature. Your browser controls permission. Alternatively, you can select a local photo. Camera frames and selected photos are processed in your browser for barcode detection; Tallysprig does not deliberately transmit those images to its hosting or product-lookup services. The decoded barcode may be sent to Open Food Facts when you request a lookup.

4. Exports and imports

CSV meal exports and JSON backups are generated locally and downloaded to your device. A full JSON backup can include your optional health profile and macro references, so it may contain more sensitive information than a meal CSV. Importing a file reads it locally in the browser. We do not operate a backend that receives these files. These files contain sensitive meal information. Anyone with a copy may be able to read it. Do not upload personal backups to public repositories.

5. Retention and deletion

Meal history and custom foods remain in local storage until removed by you or cleared by your browser/device. You can delete just the optional health profile and macro references from Health Mode settings without deleting meals, or delete all local Tallysprig data. The barcode-lookup cache is limited to 50 recent products, generally expires after 30 days, and may be cleared by the browser. Document acknowledgments remain locally until deleted or invalidated by a version update. From the Settings & data screen, you can download a complete backup or delete all Tallysprig local data. Clearing browser/site storage can also erase it; deletion cannot be undone if you have no backup.

6. Security and data limitations

Website delivery uses HTTPS when published on GitHub Pages. Browser local storage is not encrypted by Tallysprig, and this prototype does not provide account access controls, secure cloud backup, or a guarantee that device data cannot be lost. Tallysprig does not include advertising, analytics tracking, or sale of meal records in its current code. A third-party service may have its own logs and policies.

7. Children's data and future changes

Tallysprig is a prototype. Age and other profile information are optional and entered only when Health Mode is enabled; no numerical calorie-need estimate is generated for users under 18. Any wider release intended for children or teenagers requires further review of applicable privacy and consent laws. If features or third-party services change, this policy must be updated. When the document's version changes, the app asks users to acknowledge it again.

8. Contact and project operator

Before wider public release: the app operator must provide a clear identity and working contact method for privacy questions. This prototype does not yet contain a verified privacy contact. Do not represent this document as professionally reviewed or legally sufficient for commercial launch.

These documents describe V6's current implementation. They must be updated if the app's data practices or health features change. Before broader public use, provide an identifiable operator and working privacy contact, and obtain appropriate legal review.
