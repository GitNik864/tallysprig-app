Medical & Nutrition Disclaimer

Version 1.0 · Effective October 19, 2026 · For the Tallysprig V4 prototype

1. Informational use only

Tallysprig is a food-journaling tool. It is not a healthcare provider and does not provide medical advice, diagnoses, treatments, or personalized nutrition prescriptions. Logged calories and nutrients are informational estimates, not instructions about how much a person should eat.

2. Nutrition figures can be inaccurate

Built-in entries are approximate. Scanned information comes from a community-maintained database (Open Food Facts), where product names, servings, nutrients, and allergen information can be incomplete or incorrect. Manufacturing, recipe, and labeling changes may make any entry outdated. Check product packaging directly, including allergen labels; do not rely on Tallysprig for allergy safety.

3. Daily reference

The optional daily energy reference is a user-entered informational number, not a medical recommendation or enforced limit. The app does not determine what is appropriate for your age, growth, activity, or health circumstances. People who are growing, training, pregnant, managing medical conditions, or concerned about nutrition should consult an appropriately qualified healthcare professional for individualized advice.

4. Do not delay care

Do not use Tallysprig to make medical decisions or delay professional evaluation. If you experience a health concern, consult an appropriate clinician. For emergencies, contact local emergency services.

5. Scope of acknowledgment

By checking the acknowledgment in Tallysprig, you confirm that you have read this notice. Checking it does not waive legal rights, guarantee health outcomes, or substitute for professional care. The notice is separate from the Privacy Policy and may be revised as the app evolves.

These documents describe V4's current implementation. They must be updated if the app's data practices or health features change. Before broader public use, provide an identifiable operator and working privacy contact, and obtain appropriate legal review.
