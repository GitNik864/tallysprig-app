Medical & Nutrition Disclaimer

Version 1.3 · Updated October 20, 2026 · For the Tallysprig V7 with Health Mode 6.1 enhancement

1. Informational use only

Tallysprig is a food and strength-workout journaling tool. It is not a healthcare provider and does not provide medical advice, diagnoses, treatments, or personalized nutrition prescriptions. Logged calories and nutrients are informational estimates, not instructions about how much a person should eat.

2. Nutrition figures can be inaccurate

Built-in entries are approximate. Scanned information comes from a community-maintained database (Open Food Facts), where product names, servings, nutrients, and allergen information can be incomplete or incorrect. Manufacturing, recipe, and labeling changes may make any entry outdated. Check product packaging directly, including allergen labels; do not rely on Tallysprig for allergy safety.

3. Daily reference

The optional daily energy reference is a user-entered informational number, not a medical recommendation or enforced limit. Health Mode optionally provides adult-only estimated maintenance energy ranges based on entered profile data and broad activity categories. These are uncertain population-based estimates, not individualized targets. Users under 18 do not receive numerical calorie recommendations. User-entered macro references and energy references are informational and never hard limits; the app never blocks logging foods. People who are growing, training, pregnant, managing medical conditions, or concerned about nutrition should consult an appropriately qualified healthcare professional for individualized advice.

4. Workout records and safety

The Fitness tab records workouts; it does not prescribe loads, frequency, exercise techniques, or safe training intensity. Recorded weights and performance are user-entered and may be inaccurate. Exercise can involve risks. People who are young, new to resistance training, returning from injury, or managing health conditions should seek appropriate coaching and professional guidance. The app does not use workout data to deduct calories from food intake or set exercise requirements.

5. Do not delay care

Do not use Tallysprig to make medical decisions or delay professional evaluation. If you experience a health concern, consult an appropriate clinician. For emergencies, contact local emergency services.

6. Scope of acknowledgment

By checking the acknowledgment in Tallysprig, you confirm that you have read this notice. Checking it does not waive legal rights, guarantee health outcomes, or substitute for professional care. The notice is separate from the Privacy Policy and may be revised as the app evolves.

Optional adult macro examples (Health Mode 6.1)

For users who enter a complete adult profile, Tallysprig can display approximate gram-based macronutrient references based on an estimated adult maintenance-energy range and an illustrative 20% protein / 50% carbohydrate / 30% fat distribution. These percentages are one example within published adult acceptable macronutrient distribution ranges, not personalized medical, dietary, or athletic advice. A user must choose to apply them. The values are informational references, not upper limits. For users under 18, Tallysprig does not generate numeric calorie or macro targets. Never rely on these estimates to decide whether to skip food or disregard hunger, growth, or health needs.

These documents describe V7's current implementation. They must be updated if the app's data practices or health features change. Before broader public use, provide an identifiable operator and working privacy contact, and obtain appropriate legal review.
